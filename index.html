About me

From building software to securing it.

I'm a Master's student in Cybersecurity at Université Badji Mokhtar, Annaba. I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Web & Mobile), so I know how applications are built, which helps me see where they break.

In the lab I work with Kali Linux, virtual machines and isolated networks to practice reconnaissance, scanning and vulnerability analysis. I also study machine-learning-based intrusion detection on public datasets such as UNSW-NB15, TON-IoT and CIC-IDS2017.

My 2024 internship at BIG INFORMATIQUE and my bachelor project Psyra, a mental health platform, taught me to ship real products. I speak Arabic, French, English and German.
